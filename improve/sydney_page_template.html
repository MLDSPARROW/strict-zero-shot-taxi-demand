Sydney Taxi Demand

Expected taxi pickups in 401 suburbs within 25 km of the city centre, for any day and half-hour of the week.

An estimate, not a count. A model trained on taxi data from Chicago, New York and San Francisco, together with Sydney's public map data, produced these numbers. No Sydney taxi data was used, so they have not been checked against real trips.

How to read this

  • Each number is the expected number of taxi pickups in that suburb in that half-hour. It is an average, so a real half-hour can be higher or lower.
  • The pattern across suburbs and times is the model's estimate. The total is yours to set: the default of trips a day comes from a rule that uses public-transport commuters in the 2021 Census, taken on a lockdown day, so it is probably too low.
  • It counts taxi-style pickups as recorded in the three training cities, not rideshare. Outer Sydney beyond 25 km from the city centre (Penrith, Campbelltown, the Hawkesbury) is not covered.
